PREAMBLE:

WHEREAS, the Infrastructure Megaproject corridor comprising Bengaluru Peripheral Ring Road (PRR Phase-1) and BMRCL Airport Metro Link Phase 2A has been declared a Vital Public Purpose Utility under Karnataka Act No. 38 of 2012; and whereas certain private survey numbers including Survey No. 48/2A of Bellandur Village, Varthur Hobli, Bengaluru East Taluk (measuring 1 Acre 17 Guntas) are subject to ad-interim status-quo orders in OS No. 412/2024 pending on the file of Prl. Senior Civil Judge;

In exercise of powers conferred under Section 67 of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964, the Government hereby issues the following binding statutory directives to the designated district authorities:
