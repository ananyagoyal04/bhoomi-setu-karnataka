Permitted Civic Amenity Uses

local_hospital

Pursuant to the Karnataka Land Revenue Rules (Section 94 & Section 71) and the Karnataka Public Lands (Allotment for Public Amenities) Guidelines, this parcel may be leased strictly for the following approved public health functions:

health_and_safety

Primary Healthcare Centre

Outpatient and inpatient 24x7 public dispensary with subsidized or free diagnostic consultation.

Priority Code: PHC-A1
chips

Non-profit Diagnostic Facility

Advanced pathology, radiography, and community screening center accessible to low-income residents.

Priority Code: DGC-B2
pregnant_woman

Community Maternity Clinic

Maternal welfare, neonatal primary care, and child immunization facility in accordance with NHM norms.

Priority Code: MAT-C1
info
Statutory Prohibitions: Commercial subletting, purely private fee-paying nursing homes without community quota, residential quarters exceeding 10% of built-up area for essential medical staff, and commercial retail pharmacies are expressly forbidden.
Applicant Qualification

Plain-Language Eligibility Checklist

Every applicant entity must verify their compliance with all four statutory requirements before submitting an application dossier to the Deputy Commissioner:

  • check

    1. Registered Entity in Karnataka for 3+ Years

    The applicant must be a legally constituted charitable trust, registered society under the Karnataka Societies Registration Act 1960, or non-profit healthcare cooperative functioning continuously in Karnataka for not less than 36 continuous calendar months.

    Required proof: Certificate of Incorporation / Registered Trust Deed
  • check

    2. Audited Financial Statements (Last 3 Years)

    Fully audited balance sheets and Income & Expenditure accounts certified by a Chartered Accountant for FY 2021-22, FY 2022-23, and FY 2023-24, demonstrating verified financial capability to execute the intended public hospital project without commercial speculation.

    Required proof: CA-certified balance sheets with UDIN
  • check

    3. Valid Section 12A and 80G IT Registration

    Active income tax exemption recognition under Section 12A / 12AB and approval under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961, issued by the Commissioner of Income Tax.

    Required proof: Form 10AC issued by the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT)
  • check

    4. Irrevocable Lease Undertaking of Non-Transferability

    Execution of a statutory affidavit on ₹500 e-stamp paper declaring that the 30-year leasehold rights shall not be sold, sub-leased, mortgaged (save for state scheduled bank project loans), or assigned to third parties under any circumstances.

    Required proof: Notarized Stamp Affidavit (Schedule-IV Annexure)
