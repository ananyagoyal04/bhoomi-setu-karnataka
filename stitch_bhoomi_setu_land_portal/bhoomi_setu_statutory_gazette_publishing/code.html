FINAL DECLARATION UNDER SECTION 19(1) OF THE RIGHT TO FAIR COMPENSATION AND TRANSPARENCY IN LAND ACQUISITION, REHABILITATION AND RESETTLEMENT ACT, 2013
Notification No: RD-LAQ-SH17-2025-09/BLR  |  Dated: 14th March 2025

Whereas, vide Preliminary Notification No. RD-LAQ-SH17-2024-112, dated 18-09-2024 published in Karnataka State Gazette Extraordinary No. 891 Part IV-A, it was notified that the lands specified in the Schedule hereunder were likely to be needed for a public purpose, namely: Four-Laning and Expressway Geometric Modernization of State Highway 17 (Hubballi-Dharwad Bypass Corridor).

And whereas, the Land Acquisition Collector & Special Deputy Commissioner (LAQ), Dharwad, has conducted summary inquiries under Section 15 of the said Act, considered all statutory objections filed by interested persons within the prescribed 60-day limitation period, and submitted his report to the State Government.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 19 of the Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013 (Central Act 30 of 2013), the Government of Karnataka hereby solemnly declares that the land parcels specified in the Schedule below, measuring an aggregate of 148 Acres 24 Guntas (148.60 Acres), are urgently required for the said public purpose.
